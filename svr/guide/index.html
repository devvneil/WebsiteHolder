How this concept was made

This SVR Robotics concept was designed and built end-to-end by Claude Fable (Anthropic's Claude 5 model) in one autonomous session — research, art direction, copy, code. It follows two earlier demonstration sites (AURELIA and TANGERINE PRESS) built with the same method.

The Brief

Redesign svrrobotics.com — a Pune robotics & automation company with defence, nuclear, space, education and VR/AR simulation work — as something people haven't seen. The company's public data (products, divisions, client institutions, address) was gathered from its public listings; the original site blocks automated access, so all visuals here are code, with a clean slot to drop in the real logo and photographs.

1 · The signature: a real robot on the page

The hero contains a 3-link robotic arm drawn live in SVG and solved with FABRIK inverse kinematics (forward-and-backward reaching, 8 iterations per frame). Move your cursor into the cell and the arm enters TEACH mode and follows your hand; leave it alone and it returns to AUTO, running an endless six-phase pick-and-place cycle — hover, descend, grip, traverse, lower, release. The target is servo-lagged (an exponential smoother) so the motion feels mechanical rather than magnetic, and the telemetry panel streams real joint angles θ1/θ2/θ3 and TCP coordinates computed from the solved pose at 12 Hz.

2 · A design system from the factory floor

The visual language borrows from machine HMIs and engineering drawings: a blueprint grid with registration crosses, corner brackets on the robot cell, a slow CRT scanline, status LEDs, a conveyor-belt ticker, and a contact card styled as a teach pendant. Palette: graphite #0D0F12, safety orange #FF6A00, telemetry cyan #38D6E0 — the colors of an actual robot cell.

3 · Type

Chakra Petch (a squared technical face designed in Thailand) for display, Space Grotesk for body, IBM Plex Mono for telemetry and labels. All self-hosted as woff2.

4 · Content from the real company

Divisions, products and the client wall (VSSC/ISRO, BARC, SAC, IPR, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, RRCAT, C-DAC, NIT Jamshedpur, AllyGrow, Army Simulator Development Division…) come from SVR's public profiles. Copy was rewritten in a sharper voice — machines that do the impossible, daily.

5 · Zero-asset discipline

Like its sister sites, this page ships no image files: the arm, icons, logo mark, textures and ornaments are all SVG and CSS. Faster, sharper on every screen, and trivially themeable. The real photographs and logo can be dropped into assets/ and referenced without touching the design system.

6 · Process

  1. Research the company from public sources; extract real data.
  2. Pick one signature interaction that could only belong to this company (the IK arm).
  3. Commit a palette and type system as CSS variables before layout.
  4. Build static HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks, no build step.
  5. Render in a headless browser and iterate three passes at desktop + phone widths.
  6. Deploy static files to Netlify with a /guide route like this one.
