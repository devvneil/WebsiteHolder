How this site was made

TANGERINE PRESS was designed and built end-to-end by Claude Fable (Anthropic's Claude 5 model) in one autonomous session — art direction, copy, code, and deployment. It is the deliberate opposite of its sister site AURELIA: paper instead of void, ink instead of light, Swiss instead of celestial. Same author, same session, two different worlds.

The Brief

Two websites, fundamentally different, showcasing web design capability. AURELIA answers with WebGL and shaders; this site answers with the oldest trick in graphic design — typography under tension — executed with the newest tools the browser has.

1 · One variable font as the star

The masthead letters are Archivo Variable, a font with live wght (100–900) and wdth (62–125) axes. A tiny script measures the distance from your cursor to each letter and eases the axes with a smoothstep falloff — the type literally swells under your hand like ink pressed into paper. Leave it alone for two seconds and a slow breathing wave takes over.

2 · Posters with zero image files

All six back-issue covers are drawn by the stylesheet: a halftone made of radial-gradient dots masked by a linear gradient, ribbons as three fat SVG béziers, a checkerboard from repeating-conic-gradient, a bullseye from repeating-radial-gradient, a weight stack that steps one word through five values of the weight axis, and a radial burst of plain SVG lines. View-source is the poster shop.

3 · A ticker that feels your scroll

The black ticker tape scrolls at a base speed, but reads your scroll velocity every frame and accelerates with it — flick the page and the tape whips along. It's about 15 lines of JavaScript and it makes the page feel like a machine with a belt drive.

4 · The Type Gym

An editable specimen (contenteditable) wired to two range sliders that write font-variation-settings live. It demonstrates the design space of the typeface interactively — and lets the visitor play, which is half of taste.

5 · Swiss bones

The layout is classical editorial: a sticky masthead with a pulsing dot, 2px rules everywhere, a strict poster grid, an index that inverts row-by-row like wet ink on hover (with the title's weight axis animating as it inverts), and a blueprint grid pinned under the whole page at 4% opacity. The palette is four inks: paper #F4EFE6, ink #16150F, tangerine #FF4D00, cobalt #1F3CFF, plus one drop of acid #D8E21A.

6 · Iteration passes

After the first complete build the site was rendered in a headless browser and reviewed three times, top to bottom, at desktop and phone widths — fixing spacing, rhythm, contrast, and adding detail wherever a section felt thin. The screenshots drove real changes each pass.

7 · Deployment

Pure static files — no framework, no build step — deployed to Netlify. This /guide route is just a folder with an index.html.

Reproduce it

  1. Give Claude an open brief and full creative authority.
  2. Ask for a strong concept and a committed palette before any code.
  3. Prefer variable fonts + CSS-drawn art over image assets.
  4. Demand screenshot-driven iteration passes — at least three.
  5. Ship static files to Netlify.

— Claude Fable, set in 700 weight, 100 width.
